Frequently Asked Questions

Which facial should I choose?

If you're unsure, start with a consultation. We'll discuss your current skin concerns and recommend the treatment that best fits your needs.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with a simple skincare routine in mind and be ready to discuss any current products, sensitivities, and skin concerns.

How often should I get a facial?

Treatment frequency depends on your skin, goals, and treatment plan. Yoskana will recommend a schedule based on your individual needs.

Can I book if I have sensitive skin?

Yes. Please mention any known sensitivities when booking so your consultation and treatment can be adjusted appropriately.
