Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right service?

Start with a consultation if you're unsure. We'll discuss your skin or hair concerns, current routine, and personal goals to help you explore suitable care options.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with a simple skincare routine in mind and be ready to discuss any current products, sensitivities, and skin concerns.

How often should I get a facial?

Treatment frequency depends on your skin, goals, and treatment plan. YosCaNa will recommend a schedule based on your individual needs.

Can I book if I have sensitive skin?

Please let us know about any sensitivities, allergies, or existing skin conditions before your appointment. We'll discuss suitable cosmetic care options and whether medical guidance is needed before a treatment.

What is a hair porosity assessment?

A hair porosity assessment explores how your hair absorbs and retains moisture. It can help guide your choice of moisturizing products, hair oils, and everyday hair-care routines.

Are herbal hair treatments and oils personalized for my hair?

Yes. Our herbal hair-care preparations and customized oil options are selected based on your hair characteristics, preferences, and care goals. Please share any ingredient allergies or sensitivities during your consultation.
